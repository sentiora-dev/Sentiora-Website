/ Capabilities

Your document, still yours.

DocPilot is designed to support careful work. It helps people understand, compare and refine a document while keeping every suggestion visible and optional.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Read beyond the surface

Identify structure, decisions, open questions and important relationships across long documents.

02 / REFINE

Improve with context

Receive focused suggestions that respect the surrounding material and the document’s original purpose.

03 / CONTROL

Every change stays visible

Review, accept or reject assistance without surrendering ownership of the final text.

/ Release
